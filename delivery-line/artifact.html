Delivery Line

A phone number for your home that handles delivery drivers. Record how you guide them in, once. Then talk to the agent the way a driver would — and listen to it answer.

What it is allowed to do

  • answerThe driver's question is covered by your recording.
  • repeat_simplerHe did not understand. Same fact, shorter words.
  • escalateNot in your recording, security refuses him, a payment or order problem, or he asks for you directly — your phone rings.
  • end_callHe says it is delivered.

The agent is told never to invent a gate, floor or landmark. If it is not in your recording, it escalates rather than guesses — that constraint is the product.
